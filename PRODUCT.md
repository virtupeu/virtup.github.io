# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners and operations leads at small and mid-size businesses in Europe who don't have (or don't want) an in-house IT department. They arrive skeptical of agency fluff, want proof of competence fast, and decide with one question: "will these people actually pick up the phone when something breaks?"

## Product Purpose

VirtUp's single-page marketing site. It sells managed IT services: cloud infrastructure, networks, security, helpdesk, plus AI agents and automation. Success = a visitor sends the contact form. The page must feel like it was built by people who run production systems for a living.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, dependable. The site demonstrates competence instead of claiming it: live-feeling dashboards, terminals that type real commands, network animations. Emotional goal: "these people have it handled."

## Anti-references

- Generic MSP/agency template sites (stock handshake photos, "WE DELIVER SOLUTIONS")
- SaaS gradient-blob landing pages with floating glass cards
- Anything that reads as AI-generated filler copy

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell** — animated ops panels and terminals carry the proof; copy stays concrete (real version numbers, real timings).
2. **One accent, spent carefully** — electric azure (#4f9dff) on near-black; color marks what's alive or actionable, nothing else.
3. **Quiet competence** — motion is smooth and physical (custom expo-out curves, Lenis), never bouncy or loud.
4. **Plain words** — no jargon walls, no buzzwords; copy reads like a straight answer from an engineer.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Contrast ≥ AA on body text (muted text tuned to ≥5.5:1 on bg)
- Focus-visible outlines, aria labels on decorative panels, semantic landmarks
- Note: animations are intentionally forced on (`motionOK = true`) per owner decision; their OS falsely reported reduced-motion and killed all animation. Keep it that way unless the owner says otherwise.
